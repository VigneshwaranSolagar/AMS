Auto Message System (AMS)

VinSolve respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information processed through Auto Message System (AMS).

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored and protected when you use AMS.

By installing, activating or using AMS, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

AMS may process information required to provide software functionality and business communication services.

  • Customer contact information such as names, phone numbers and communication details.
  • Message templates, campaign information and communication history.
  • Software activation information and license details.
  • Installation information required for license validation.
  • Application configuration and system settings.

2. License Activation Information

AMS uses an offline license activation mechanism to validate authorized software usage.

During activation, the software may generate a unique Installation ID (Machine Fingerprint).

This information is used only for license generation, activation validation and protection against unauthorized software usage.

3. Use of Information

Information processed by AMS may be used for the following purposes:

  • Provide and maintain AMS functionality.
  • Process software activation and license management.
  • Support customer communication workflows.
  • Improve software reliability and performance.
  • Provide technical support services.
  • Protect against unauthorized access and misuse.

4. Customer Data Ownership

All business data entered into AMS remains the property of the respective customer or organization.

VinSolve does not claim ownership rights over customer contacts, messages, campaigns or business information.

Customers are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights and permissions to process such information.

5. Data Storage

AMS is designed to operate primarily using customer-controlled systems and storage environments.

Customer data storage location depends on the deployment configuration and infrastructure used by the customer.

Customers are responsible for maintaining appropriate backup procedures and access controls.

6. Data Security

VinSolve implements reasonable technical measures to protect software licensing information and customer support data.

  • Protection of license files against unauthorized copying.
  • Secure handling of support communications.
  • Software security improvements through updates.
  • Access control practices for administrative functions.
Important

Customers are responsible for securing their own devices, network environment, login credentials and business data.

7. Third-Party Services

AMS may integrate with third-party communication platforms, including WhatsApp and other external services.

VinSolve does not control the privacy practices, availability, policies, pricing or security measures of third-party service providers.

Customers are responsible for reviewing and complying with the privacy policies and terms of service of such third-party platforms.

8. Communication Data

AMS may store communication-related information required for business operations, including message templates, campaign details and delivery-related information.

Customers are responsible for ensuring that communication activities comply with applicable laws, regulations and platform policies.

9. Data Sharing

VinSolve does not sell, rent or trade customer business information.

Information may only be disclosed when necessary:

  • To provide requested technical support.
  • To comply with legal obligations or lawful requests.
  • To protect the rights, security or integrity of VinSolve, AMS or its users.

10. Data Retention

VinSolve retains information only for as long as necessary to provide services, maintain licensing records, support customers and comply with legal obligations.

Customers may request clarification regarding retained information by contacting VinSolve.

11. Customer Responsibilities

Customers using AMS are responsible for:

  • Obtaining appropriate consent before sending messages.
  • Maintaining accurate customer information.
  • Protecting access credentials.
  • Maintaining backups of business information.
  • Following applicable privacy and communication regulations.

12. User Rights

Depending on applicable laws, users may have rights regarding their personal information, including:

  • Requesting access to personal information.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion where legally applicable.
  • Requesting information about data processing practices.

Requests may be submitted using the contact information provided below.

13. Children's Privacy

AMS is intended for business and organizational use.

VinSolve does not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

14. Policy Updates

VinSolve may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in software functionality, legal requirements or business practices.

Updated versions will be published with a revised effective date.

Continued use of AMS after updates indicates acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
